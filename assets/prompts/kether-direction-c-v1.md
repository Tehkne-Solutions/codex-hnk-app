# Kether Visual Direction C — Atziluth Liminal

Status: concept v1
Use: Visual Bible exploration only; not canonical until explicit approval.

## Core idea
Kether as a liminal sacred architecture suspended in Atziluth: vast silence, radiant threshold, cosmic but disciplined, immersive without fantasy excess. This direction is the most atmospheric of the three.

## Mandatory content
- Today screen language
- Day card
- Kether key art
- active Tree of Life node
- audio player
- encrypted journal shell
- Portal 36 transition motif
- black void and spatial depth
- white light emerging from a crown/threshold
- restrained gold integration lines
- rare ritual blue reserved for transition/portal energy
- primordial geometry embedded in architecture

## Forbidden
- galaxies as generic wallpaper
- angels/figures invented without canonical approval
- random occult glyphs
- psychedelic chaos
- horror or demonic imagery
- baroque fantasy temples
- excessive fog hiding usability
- decorative UI with poor mobile legibility

## Visual language
Monumental void architecture, reflective black stone and dark glass, floating circular planes, controlled mist, hard shafts of white light, sparse gold seams, immense scale with calm silence. Sacred minimalism meets cinematic spatial design.

## UI
UI feels embedded in the environment: floating dark panels, soft glass depth, minimal labels, strong touch targets, state light rather than ornament. Keep the Today view calm and cognitively light.

## Continuity
Kether = crown, origin, fertile void, silence, beginning, potential, primordial geometry. Atziluth should feel like a world of emanation, while all spiritual hierarchy remains subordinate to divine authority defined by the Codex.
