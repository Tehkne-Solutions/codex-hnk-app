# Kether Visual Direction A — Coroa do Vazio

Status: concept v1
Use: Visual Bible exploration only; not canonical until explicit approval.

## Core idea
A sacred, architectural, contemporary visual system where Kether appears as the first emergence of ordered light from fertile blackness. Minimal, severe, luminous, precise.

## Mandatory content
- visual language for Today screen
- Day card
- Kether key art
- active Tree of Life node
- audio player
- encrypted journal shell
- Portal 36 transition motif
- deep black foundation
- white radiance as primary state light
- restrained gold for authority/integration
- primordial circles, axes and crown geometry

## Forbidden
- random occult symbols
- invented sigils presented as canonical
- gothic fantasy ornament
- demonic imagery
- horror
- pseudo-scientific anatomy
- generic SaaS dashboard
- excessive particle noise
- unreadable decorative typography

## Visual language
Premium ritual instrument. Obsidian-black architectural void, matte and polished mineral surfaces, extremely controlled volumetric white light, thin gold conductive lines, concentric geometry, monumental negative space, subtle micro-particles. High-end product design fused with sacred architecture.

## UI
HUD ritual, restrained density, strong hierarchy, luminous state indicators, generous black space, geometric cards, circular progress motifs. No decorative text needed in concept imagery.

## Continuity
Kether = crown, origin, fertile void, silence, beginning, potential, primordial geometry. God remains above symbol/operator; imagery must not visually deify interface, angel, operator or technology.
