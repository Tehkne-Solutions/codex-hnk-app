# Kether Visual Direction B — Códice Operacional

Status: concept v1
Use: Visual Bible exploration only; not canonical until explicit approval.

## Core idea
Kether as a living initiatory operating system: sacred instrumentation, precise ritual telemetry, codex intelligence and navigable cosmology. More technical than Direction A, without becoming generic software.

## Mandatory content
- Today screen language
- Day card
- Kether key art
- active Tree of Life node
- audio/sintonizador player
- encrypted journal shell
- Portal 36 transition motif
- dark field with white/gold instrumentation
- subtle Atziluth depth
- circles, grids, orbital traces and crown-node geometry

## Forbidden
- cyberpunk neon overload
- random occult glyphs
- fake scientific scans
- generic analytics dashboard
- gamer HUD clutter
- horror/demonic motifs
- religious iconography not already approved
- fantasy steampunk machinery

## Visual language
Black glass, smoked metal, fine engraved geometry, floating translucent panes, luminous white vectors, restrained gold status accents, occasional ritual blue only as contextual transition energy. Feels like a precision observatory for consciousness and ritual practice.

## UI
Instrument-grade controls, radial meters, elegant thin-line telemetry, semantic glows for active/unlocked/completed states, clear mobile hierarchy. Interfaces should feel touchable and calm, not busy.

## Continuity
Kether = origin and crown. The system visualizes HNK states and progression; visual effects are symbolic/operational representations, not claims of external scientific mechanism.
